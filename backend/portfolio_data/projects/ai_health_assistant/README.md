# AI Health Assistant

## Overview
Inspired by the lack of direct, plain-language health and wellness resources for users in India, Jeswar built the AI Health Assistant. The app acts as a general Q&A chatbot that translates complex medical terminology into simple wellness tips.

## Core Features
- **Symptom Translation**: Explains medical terms in plain, easy-to-understand language.
- **Evidence-Based Wellness Advice**: Focuses on diet, basic exercises, and healthy habits.
- **Safety Disclaimers**: Displays visible prompts reminding users that the tool is for general guidance and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
